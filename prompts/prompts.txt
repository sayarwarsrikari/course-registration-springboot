PROMPT 2 - Edge Cases

I'm working on a course registration system. Help me think through the edge cases before I write any logic.

What happens if a student who is already enrolled tries to enroll again?

What happens if a student tries to join the waitlist but they're already on it?

What happens if the first person on the waitlist doesn't meet the prerequisites when a seat opens up? Do they lose their spot or stay in line?

What happens if a student tries to drop a course they were never in?

What if they drop and then try to drop again?

Can a student be enrolled and on the waitlist at the same time? How do we stop that?

What if the waitlist is empty when someone drops?

PROMPT 3 - Test Cases

I need to figure out what tests to write for my enrollment service. Give me the scenarios I need to cover.

I know I need the happy path where someone enrolls successfully. What else?

What should happen when the course is full and someone tries to enroll?

What is the test that proves waitlist order is correct and not random?

What test proves that when someone drops, the right person from the waitlist gets promoted?

What test proves the enrolled count never goes above capacity no matter what?

What tests cover the failure cases like missing prerequisites, duplicate requests, and dropping when not enrolled?
